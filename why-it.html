Why IT?

I already did minimal IT back at home.
Opening stuff up, and looking inside to see how does it work.

It started with my weird curiosity.

Computers have always been more than something I use. I wanted to understand what happens when I sit in front of it doing my homework or playing video games, and how software and networks work together.

What I know about the field

From the outside and from my family's perspective, IT looks like one job, but it's really a lot of different ones. People write software, keep networks and servers running, protect them from attacks or help the people who use them. So far I've mostly seen the hardware and server side, because that's what I did myself. I but I know there is a lot more that I don't know.

What I bring from my previous experiences

I have spent countless hours on troubleshooting computers, servers and basically any other dying electronics that I could find lying around no matter how stupid it was which boosted my patience by a huge amount.

What I expect from HZ

What I want to learn
